Run The Panel

Five Steps. No Shortcuts.

Every candidate goes through the same disciplined pipeline — no step is skipped, no score is guessed.

01

Profile Builder

Resume and interview transcript are parsed into one shared factual profile: skills, experience, and every explicit claim — tagged with its source quote.

02

Independent Agents

Four agents evaluate the candidate in complete isolation. No agent sees another's opinion. Every verdict must be backed by a real quote.

03

The Debate

Agents are shown each other's opinions and respond directly — agreeing, disagreeing, or revising their own view based on what they hear.

04

Decision Synthesis

A dedicated reasoning step weighs evidence strength and confidence — not an average of four scores.

05

Final Report

Recommendation, confidence, strengths, concerns — and any disagreement the panel never fully resolved.

No agent ever sees another's conclusion until the debate stage begins.
